Dear New Students (October 2026 Intake),

Please be informed that this timetable group consists of students from Year 2, Year 3, Year 4, as well as new students like you.

Whenever I post announcements or timetable updates in this group, please read them carefully. If the information is not relevant to your intake or programme, you may simply ignore it, as some announcements are intended for your seniors.

Please take note of the following important points:

1. Foreign Language and KoQ

New students are not required to register for Foreign Language and KoQ courses at this stage. Please follow your respective curriculum structure.

2. Check Your Curriculum Structure and Timetable

Please refer to your curriculum structure and check the timetable carefully. The courses listed in your timetable are the courses you need to register for. If a course is not listed in your timetable, it may be intended for your seniors, even if I share an announcement about it in this group.

3. Section Changes Are Not Allowed Without Permission

New students are strictly not allowed to change their assigned sections on their own. Please follow the sections assigned to you and do not simply switch to another section without obtaining permission from the Faculty.

Lecturers generally expect new students to attend the sections assigned to them. Unauthorised section changes may cause confusion and create problems with class attendance and course registration.

If you have any issues or genuine reasons for requesting a section change, please seek advice and obtain approval from the Faculty before making any changes.

Please follow this instruction strictly.

4. Ask Questions in This Group

If you have any questions or uncertainties, you are welcome to ask them in this group. You can also learn from other students here instead of relying solely on UTM Chat.

Please make it a habit to refer to your curriculum structure and timetable before asking about course registration. This will help you identify the courses relevant to your intake and avoid unnecessary confusion.

Thank you for your attention and cooperation. Kindly take note of the above and follow the instructions accordingly.
