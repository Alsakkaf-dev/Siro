NNeed

Students buy once, use once, and have nowhere safe to pass it on

29 respondents

UTM courses require specialised, single-use items — lab coats, scientific calculators, drawing sets, presentation attire — that lose all value after one semester. The only sharing channel that exists today is an unverified WhatsApp or Telegram group: no identity, no reputation, and no record of who holds what. Lenders are therefore afraid to lend, and the items sit unused in dorm rooms until they are thrown away.

AApproach

Every loan is a state machine; every handover is a signature

3 subsystems · 9 modules

Identity & User Management

Mohammed Alsakkaf & Muaz Ibne Ahmed
  • Authentication
  • Profile & Trust Score
  • Activity Dashboard

Only @utm.my and @graduate.utm.my addresses may register. Passwords are bcrypt-hashed; sessions are signed JWTs. A 5-star Trust Score is recomputed from completed loans only.

Resource Catalog & Discovery

Muaz Ibne Ahmed
  • Item Listing & Management
  • Category & Condition Metadata
  • Location-Based Filter

Every listing carries a server-enforced category and condition. Borrowers narrow by residential college and faculty, and sort by true great-circle distance over campus coordinates — no third-party map service.

Transaction & Handover

Mohammed Mohsen Mohammed Alsakkaf
  • Request & Approval Workflow
  • QR Verification
  • Moderation & Reporting

A request atomically locks the item, so two students can never claim the same tool. Every state change is appended to an immutable log, and the admin portal is gated behind an authenticator code.

Proof of physical handover
UTMB.<payload>.<signature>

The payload binds the transaction, the borrower, a random nonce and the issue time. The signature is HMAC-SHA256 over it, computed with a server-held secret and re-verified on every scan. Tokens expire after 24 hours, defeating replay of a captured code, and every attempt — success, expired, wrong transaction, already used — is written to a forensic scan log.

BBenefit

What the community gets

Borrowers · lenders · the university

Borrowers stop buying

The RM50–RM150 a student currently spends each year on single-use items stays in their pocket. Items are found by college and faculty, so collection is a walk, not a trip — and dorm rooms stay clear of things used once a year.

Lenders lend without fear

The counterpart is a verified UTM member with a Trust Score that had to be earned. If a dispute arises, there is cryptographic proof of both the handover and the return, timestamped — not a screenshot.

The university keeps control

The email gate keeps non-affiliates off campus trade entirely. Moderation is MFA-gated and every decision is permanently audited, so student welfare cases can be reviewed rather than argued.

Every item borrowed instead of bought is one fewer purchased, and one fewer discarded at the end of semester. The platform turns that into reportable data for UTM’s Smart & Green Campus agenda and UN SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production. In our survey, 69% of students said an environmental-impact screen would make them want to share more.
