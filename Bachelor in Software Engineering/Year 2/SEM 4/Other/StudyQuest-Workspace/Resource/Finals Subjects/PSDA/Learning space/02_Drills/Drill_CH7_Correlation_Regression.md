# Drill — CH7: Correlation & Regression (8 problems)

> Modeled on his Q4/Q5 patterns (15 + 15 marks every year). Ramp: P1–P2 easy r, P3 Spearman, P4 significance test, P5–P8 regression at exam level. Answers: `Drill_CH7_Correlation_Regression_ANSWERS.md`.

---

**P1 (Pearson r, small n — warm-up).**
Hours studied (x) and quiz score (y) for 5 students:

| x | 2 | 4 | 5 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| y | 55 | 62 | 70 | 80 | 86 |

(i) Find Σx, Σy, Σx², Σy², Σxy. (3 m)
(ii) Calculate r and interpret. (5 m)

**P2 (Pearson r, n = 8 — style of 2021/22 Q4 / 2018/19 Q4a).**
Weekly ad spend in RM hundreds (x) and sales in RM thousands (y) for 8 weeks:

| x | 3 | 6 | 4 | 7 | 9 | 5 | 8 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| y | 12 | 15 | 13 | 18 | 22 | 14 | 19 | 10 |

(i) Identify which correlation coefficient is suitable for this data. Justify. (2 m)
(ii) Calculate r. (8 m)
(iii) Conclude on the relationship between ad spend and sales. (2 m)

**P3 (Spearman — style of 2015/16 Q4a).**
A product manager ranks 8 app features by perceived importance (1 = most important); the same features have measured monthly usage (thousands of sessions):

| Feature | A | B | C | D | E | F | G | H |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manager's rank | 5 | 1 | 7 | 2 | 8 | 6 | 4 | 3 |
| Usage | 4.90 | 7.00 | 5.60 | 7.30 | 5.20 | 5.73 | 6.83 | 6.50 |

(i) Rank the usage values (1 = highest usage) and calculate Spearman's r_s. (8 m)
(ii) Does r_s suggest the manager's intuition matches actual usage? (2 m)

**P4 (significance of correlation — style of 2016/17 Q5ii–iii).**
A study of n = 12 warehouses finds r = 0.72 between floor area and monthly throughput.
(i) Test at α = 0.05 whether the variables really correlate (95% confidence). (3 m)
(ii) Would the decision change at 99% confidence? (2 m)

**P5 (regression line — style of 2023/24 Q5a).**
Years of experience (x) and monthly salary in RM thousands (y) for 7 analysts:

| x | 1 | 3 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| y | 3.1 | 3.2 | 4.5 | 4.6 | 6.1 | 6.0 | 7.0 |

(i) Find Σx, Σy, Σx², Σy², Σxy. (5 m)
(ii) Find the least-squares regression equation. (4 m)
(iii) Estimate the salary of an analyst with 7 years of experience. (1 m)

**P6 (prediction + extrapolation — style of 2015/16 Q4b iii).**
Daily temperature (°C, x) and iced-drink sales (units, y) over 7 days: x: 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 30; y: 110, 125, 138, 156, 172, 190, 205. The fitted line is ŷ = −35.857 + 8.018x (you may verify).
(i) Predict sales at 25°C. (2 m)
(ii) A manager wants the prediction for a 40°C heatwave. Explain why using this equation would not be reasonable. (2 m)

**P7 (R² and interpretation — style of 2022/23 Q5e).**
For the data in P5, SST = 13.434 and SSE = 0.884.
(i) Find SSR and the coefficient of determination R². (3 m)
(ii) Interpret R² in context — what percent of the variation in salary is explained by experience? (2 m)

**P8 (slope t-test — style of 2023/24 Q5b / 2021/22 Q5b).**
For the temperature/sales data in P6, the estimated standard error of the slope is s_b1 = 0.144.
Does temperature affect iced-drink sales? Test at the 95% confidence level. (5 m)
