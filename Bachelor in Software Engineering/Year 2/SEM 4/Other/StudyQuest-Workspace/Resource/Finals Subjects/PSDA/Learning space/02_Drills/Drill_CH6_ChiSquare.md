# Drill — CH6: Chi-Square Tests (6 problems)

> Modeled on his past finals (Q3 pattern: GOF + independence, 20 marks). Ramp: P1–P2 easy GOF, P3 unequal GOF, P4–P6 independence at exam level. Answers: `Drill_CH6_ChiSquare_ANSWERS.md`.

**P1 (GOF, equal — style of 2023/24 Q3a / 2022/23 Q3a).**
A help desk logs tickets opened per weekday in a random week: Mon 52, Tue 44, Wed 38, Thu 41, Fri 55.
(i) State the hypotheses for testing that tickets are equally distributed across the 5 weekdays. (1 m)
(ii) Calculate the expected frequencies and the test statistic. (4 m)
(iii) Find the critical value at α = 0.05 and state your conclusion. (3 m)

**P2 (GOF, equal — style of 2021/22 Q3 conceptual + slides Exercise #1 die).**
A six-sided die used in a board-game app is rolled 60 times; observed faces: 1→8, 2→13, 3→7, 4→12, 5→11, 6→9. Test at α = 0.05 whether the die is fair. (6 m)

**P3 (GOF, unequal — style of 2016/17 Q4a / 2017/18 Q4a).**
A vendor claims its web traffic splits across operating systems as: Android 60%, iOS 25%, Windows 10%, Others 5%. A random sample of 500 sessions shows: Android 274, iOS 139, Windows 55, Others 32. Test the claim at α = 0.05. (10 m)

**P4 (independence 2×2 — style of slides Example 6).**
A product team samples 400 users to study churn by device type:

| | Churned | Stayed | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile | 44 | 156 | 200 |
| Desktop | 61 | 139 | 200 |
| Total | 105 | 295 | 400 |

At α = 0.05, test whether churn is related to device type. (8 m)

**P5 (independence 2×3 at α = 0.01 — style of 2021/22 Q3b).**
An e-commerce site cross-tabulates region against preferred payment method for 300 orders:

| | E-wallet | Card | Bank transfer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Urban | 62 | 48 | 40 |
| Rural | 38 | 52 | 60 |

Test at α = 0.01 whether payment preference is related to region. (12 m)

**P6 (independence 3×3 — style of 2017/18 Q4b homogeneity).**
A factory records defect type by shift for 180 defective items:

| | Scratch | Dent | Misalignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | 36 | 14 | 10 |
| Evening | 28 | 22 | 10 |
| Night | 16 | 24 | 20 |

At α = 0.05, test whether defect type is independent of shift. (12 m)
